Your gut may help your brain decide what to remember

Published: Wed, 29 Jul 2026 08:20:24 EDT — via ScienceDaily

Signals traveling from the gut to the brain may help turn meals into lasting memories. Rats showed stronger memory activity after consuming nutritious foods, but not after drinking sweet liquids with little or no nutritional value. Blocking the vagus nerve erased this effect and made it harder for the animals to remember where food had been located. Over time, diets high in fat and sugar also weakened this memory system.

Earth’s hidden “gold kitchen” lies beneath the seafloor

Published: Wed, 29 Jul 2026 00:14:05 EDT — via ScienceDaily

Deep beneath submarine volcanoes, Earth may be running a natural “gold kitchen.” By analyzing volcanic glass from the Kermadec island arc north of New Zealand, researchers found that water-rich mantle repeatedly melts beneath subduction zones, gradually concentrating gold in rising magma. The process works because intense melting breaks down sulfur-rich minerals that normally trap gold, releasing the metal into the melt.

Hidden charcoal reveals the true age of ancient cave paintings

Published: Tue, 28 Jul 2026 07:16:44 EDT — via ScienceDaily
