Diet drinks work as well as water for weight loss, major trial finds

Published: Sat, 03 Oct 2026 22:10:59 EDT — via ScienceDaily

A two-year randomized clinical trial involving 493 adults with overweight or obesity found that diet soft drinks were essentially as effective as water for long-term weight loss and weight maintenance. Participants drinking beverages sweetened with aspartame, acesulfame-K, or sucralose lost a similar amount of weight to those drinking water, while both groups also saw improvements in waist size, body composition, and several metabolic measures.

Scientists discover a human brain gene that can jump through DNA

Published: Mon, 05 Oct 2026 06:06:28 EDT — via ScienceDaily

Scientists have discovered a human gene with a remarkable double life: it appears to play an important role in brain cells while still retaining the ability to jump around the genome. Called BC200, the gene evolved millions of years ago from a mobile piece of DNA known as a transposon, but unlike most genes that arose this way, it never lost its mobility. Researchers even found BC200 embedded inside a human poxvirus, suggesting it may have jumped from infected skin cells into the virus.

Published: Mon, 05 Oct 2026 05:24:29 EDT — via ScienceDaily
