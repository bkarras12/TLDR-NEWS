Published: Fri, 25 Sep 2026 09:01:20 EDT — via ScienceDaily

CERN has started replacing some of the Large Hadron Collider’s most important magnets as part of its High-Luminosity upgrade. Published: Thu, 24 Sep 2026 09:16:41 EDT — via ScienceDaily
