## ## Trade Turmoil and Economic Consequences

### Trump's Trade Policy Shift
Donald Trump’s refusal to renew the US-Canada-Mexico Agreement (USMCA) underscores a significant turning point in North American trade relations. Instead of committing to a long-term renewal, the Trump administration has opted for annual reviews, restricting the agreement’s stability. This decision, made just as the US economy grapples with inflationary pressures, threatens to create uncertainty for businesses reliant on cross-border commerce. The potential for increased tariffs and trade disputes looms large.

This abrupt shift in trade policy comes on the heels of the US blocking a long-term renewal of its North American trade deal, which could lead to economic fragmentation just as recovery efforts are underway. For ordinary consumers, this translates into potential price increases on goods manufactured in Canada and Mexico, which could exacerbate inflationary pressures already felt at the gas pump and grocery store.
