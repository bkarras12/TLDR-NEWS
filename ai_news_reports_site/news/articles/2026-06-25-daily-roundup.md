## Global Disasters and Political Struggles

### Venezuelan Earthquake Wreaks Havoc
Venezuelans experienced terror today as two earthquakes struck the capital, first at a magnitude of 7.2 and then a 7.5 just seconds later. Residents reported panic, fearing buildings would collapse around them. The aftermath is not just a matter of infrastructure but a humanitarian crisis as aid efforts scramble to respond to the urgent needs of the affected population. The situation raises questions about the Venezuelan government’s readiness to handle such disasters, reflecting broader governance issues in a nation already facing economic collapse.

Simultaneously, Europe is experiencing a record-breaking heatwave, with countries like France, the UK, and Spain issuing red alerts as temperatures soar. This climate crisis threatens public health and exacerbates existing issues, such as energy shortages. France's reevaluation of its air conditioning policies underscores the political divide over climate adaptation strategies. As millions struggle with the sweltering heat, the call for sustainable solutions grows louder.

### U.S. Military Funding Controversy
Back in the U.S., President Trump is requesting an urgent $87 billion from Congress, primarily for military costs related to the ongoing conflict with Iran. ### School Shooting in the Philippines

### Ebola Case in France Raises Alarm
