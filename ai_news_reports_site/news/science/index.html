REM sleep paradox: Dreaming may drain the brain’s energy even as fuel supply rises

Published: Sat, 26 Sep 2026 07:30:49 EDT — via ScienceDaily

Scientists discovered a surprising energy paradox in the brain during REM sleep, the stage most closely associated with dreaming. The finding suggests that dreaming may require such intense internal processing that neurons consume energy faster than it can be replenished.
